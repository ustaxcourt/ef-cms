# Outstanding Questions

1. The spec states: "If an appearance document (Entry of Appearance or Limited
Entry of Appearance) was filed by a practitioner and it is on the pending
report, be sure that "Practitioner" is auto populated in the Filed By column when a clerk first accesses the minute sheet." The question is: are Entry of Appearance or Limited Entry of Appearance the only two document types that trigger this behavior, or do other document types available to a private practitioner making their first submission on a case also qualify? These are the other document types:

- motion to substitute parties and change caption
- notice of election to intervene
- notice of election to participate
- notice of intervention
- substitution of counsel
