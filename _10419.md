# Outstanding Questions Regarding PO Feedback

1. Feedback states: "If an appearance document (Entry of Appearance or Limited
Entry of Appearance) was filed by a practitioner and it is on the pending
report, be sure that "Practitioner" is auto populated in the Filed By column
when a clerk first accesses the minute sheet."

The question is: are Entry of Appearance or Limited Entry of Appearance the only
two document types that trigger this behavior, or do other document types
available to a private practitioner making their first submission on a case also
qualify? These are the other document types:

- motion to substitute parties and change caption
- notice of election to intervene
- notice of election to participate
- notice of intervention
- substitution of counsel

2. Feedback states: The mocks and Test Case 40 indicate that the header of the
PDF should display the following format: "Minutes of Proceedings: [City],
[State]; [Month abbreviated] [Day], [Year] - [Assigned Judge Name]". Currently,
it is displaying only the case docket number. If consolidated it displays
"Docket No.:[docket number], et al.". What are your thoughts? Is the current
display ok? Do we need to update it to what it is in the mocks?

The question is: what kind of header does the court require for a minute sheet?
